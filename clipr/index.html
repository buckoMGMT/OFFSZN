Credit-tool model: a $29/mo plan including 300 source-minutes, with further minutes bought as credits at an assumed $0.10 each — our estimate from published plan sizes, not a quoted overage rate. ClipR model: the cheapest ClipR plan whose monitored-hours allowance covers the slider.

What we changed

  • Watching is free. You're billed for monitored hours in wide bands and for clips you publish — not for every minute of footage that entered the pipe.
  • Rejected clips cost nothing. If you kill a clip in review, it never counts against your allowance. Tools that bill on ingest charge you for their own bad calls.
  • Accounts aren't a paywall. Streamers run a main, a clips channel, a vertical account and a burner. Connected destinations are unmetered from the first paid plan.
  • Overage is a soft ceiling. Cross your clip allowance and the queue keeps running at $0.35 a clip, capped at the next plan's price. You never wake up to a dead pipeline or a surprise invoice.

Questions

The things people ask before they connect a channel.

Does it post without me looking at anything?

That's the default, and it's the point. You can switch any destination to review-first, in which case clips land in a queue you swipe through on your phone. Most people run auto-post to their clips accounts and review-first on their main.

What counts as a monitored hour?

Wall-clock time your channel is live while ClipR is attached. Offline hours, standby and clips you re-cut later cost nothing. If you go over, monitoring keeps running — we bill the overage rather than dropping your stream.

What happens when a clip gets rejected?

It costs nothing. Only published clips draw down your allowance, which means our ranker being wrong is our problem, not a line on your invoice.

Can it handle gameplay, not just faces?

Yes. Full-screen game footage uses a motion and HUD-aware crop rather than speaker tracking, and can composite your webcam back in as a corner or split layout.

Who owns the clips?

You do, entirely. Exports are watermark-free from the first paid plan, and you can pull every rendered file over the API or as a bulk download at any time — including after you cancel.

What about copyright and music in my stream?

Network plans flag detected music and third-party footage before anything publishes. On lower plans the flag appears in your review queue. ClipR never edits out a claim on your behalf — that call stays yours.
