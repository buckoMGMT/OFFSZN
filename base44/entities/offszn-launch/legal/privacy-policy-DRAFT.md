## 1. Who can use OFFSZN
OFFSZN is for athletes 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from
children under 13. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 13, we
delete it. Athletes 13–17 are "minors" under this policy and have added
protections described in Section 6.

## 2. Information we collect
- **Account:** email, date of birth (to verify age), password (hashed by our
  auth provider).
- **Profile:** name/handle, sport, position, team/clan, avatar.
- **Training data:** workouts, meals/macros, weight and progress entries,
  streaks — the data you enter to use the app.
- **Activity:** posts, comments, likes, drill purchases, points earned and
  redeemed, referrals.
- **Payment:** processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers.
- **Technical:** device type, app version, IP address, and [FILL: analytics —
  e.g. PostHog] usage events.
- **Guardian data (for minors):** a parent/guardian's email and verification
  status when required (Section 6).

## 3. How we use it
To run the app, verify age, process payments and payouts, operate the points
and rewards economy, detect fraud and abuse, moderate content, provide
support, and — with your consent where required — send you email. We do not
sell your personal information.

## 4. How we share it

## 6. Minors (13–17) — added protections
- Core training features work without a guardian. Two things require a
  verified parent/guardian: purchasing coach content or contacting a coach
  directly, and competing publicly (open leaderboards, high-stakes
  challenges).
- Sweepstakes and prize promotions are limited to entrants 18+.
- We apply stricter content moderation to protect minor users.
- Guardians may request access to or deletion of their minor's data via
  privacy@offszn.app.
